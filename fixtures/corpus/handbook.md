# Fictitious Company Handbook

## Vacation Policy

Employees accrue vacation at 1.5 days per month of employment. Vacation
requests must be submitted through the portal at least 14 days before the
requested start date. Unused vacation carries over, capped at 20 days.

## Remote Work

Remote work is allowed up to three days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP sign-off and a quarterly review.

## Office Hours

The headquarters office opens at 08:00 and closes at 18:00 local time.
Badge access is available 24 hours for engineering staff.

## Expense Reimbursement

Expenses under 50 units are reimbursed without a receipt. Above that, an
itemized receipt is required. All expenses must be filed within 30 days.

## Security Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Hardware security keys are
issued to all staff and required for VPN access. Security incidents must
be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery.
