# Field Guide: Care of Indeterminate Plants

The indeterminate fern thrives in indirect light and high humidity.
Water when the top two centimeters of soil feel dry. Overwatering shows
as yellowing fronds near the base.

## Repotting

Repot every second spring using a loose, bark-heavy mix. Choose a pot
one size larger; oversized pots retain water and invite root rot.

## Common Problems

Brown crispy tips mean dry air, not underwatering. Pale fronds signal
too much direct sun. A sudden drop of all fronds usually means the
plant was moved or shocked by cold drafts.
