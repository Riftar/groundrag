# Product Notes: Cyclone Router

The Cyclone router ships in two variants. The C100 covers 80 square
meters with one mesh node, dual-band wifi, and four gigabit ports. The
C200 adds a 6 GHz band, covers 140 square meters, and includes one
additional mesh node in the box.

## Firmware

Firmware updates install automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local
time. Manual updates are available in the admin console under System.

## Warranty

The Cyclone line carries a two year limited warranty covering hardware
defects. Water damage and power surges are excluded. Warranty claims
need the serial number and proof of purchase.

## Support

Support hours are 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, Monday through Friday. The
knowledge base is available around the clock.
